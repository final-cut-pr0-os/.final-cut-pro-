## 🔧 Troubleshooting & Tips

**Q: Does this replace Final Cut Pro?**  
No. It enhances the existing installation.

**Q: Will it break with macOS Sequoia?**  
The current build is signed and notarized for macOS 14–15. Updates follow Apple's release schedule.

**Q: Proxy generation slows down playback?**  
Disable background rendering during ingest. Proxy Flow respects your render settings.

**Q: How do I reset everything?**  
Launch the Smart Assembly panel → hold Option → click "Restore Defaults." All preferences revert, no uninstall required.
